About My Shop

Our Story

My Shop was created to provide quality and affordable products for customers. It started as a simple idea to create an online shop where people can easily discover and choose products they like.

We believe that shopping should be simple, convenient, and enjoyable. Our goal is to provide good products and a pleasant experience for every customer who visits our shop.

About the Owner

My name is Jerik Angeles, and I am the owner of My Shop. I created this shop as part of my web development project and to practice my skills in creating and designing websites.

Through this shop, I want to apply what I have learned about HTML, website structure, and navigation while creating a useful and simple online shopping experience.
