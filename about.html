About My Shop

Hi! I'm Jerik Angeles

Welcome to my place guide! I am Jerik Angeles, a student and future educator who enjoys exploring interesting places and discovering destinations that are worth visiting.

I created this website to share some of the places that I find interesting and worth recommending. Through this guide, I want to help visitors discover relaxing, historical, and interesting destinations in the Philippines.

The Story Behind My Guide

I created this website as part of my web development project. It allows me to practice my skills in HTML, CSS, and website design while sharing useful information about different places.

Why I Chose This Topic

I chose this topic because exploring different places is a great way to learn outside the classroom. Historical parks and beautiful destinations can help people appreciate the history, culture, and beauty of the Philippines.
